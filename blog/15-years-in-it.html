What 15+ years in IT has taught me

Working across multinational organisations has reinforced a simple lesson: technology succeeds when people, process and service outcomes stay connected.

My experience in IT service management has taught me to value clear ownership, continual improvement, practical governance and communication that works for both technical teams and the business.

I’ll use this journal to share more lessons, observations and ideas from that journey.
