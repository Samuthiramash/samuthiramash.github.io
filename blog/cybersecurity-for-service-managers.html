Cybersecurity for service managers

Cybersecurity increasingly touches every part of service delivery. Service managers can help bridge the gap between security teams and operational teams by making security considerations visible in everyday service practices.

Incident response, change management, service continuity, access management and supplier governance all create opportunities to improve cyber resilience.

Future posts will explore these connections through practical examples and lessons from service operations.
